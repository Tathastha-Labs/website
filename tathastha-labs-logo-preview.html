Concept. The mark is a perfect circle — the literal sense of tathastha: the impartial observer. Inside, three nodes sit on an equilateral triangle around a central focal node, joined by spokes and a triangular network edge — a minimal neural graph that reads as data, model, and inference orbiting an equilibrium core. Monochrome (ink, graphite, paper, noir) keeps the system maximally neutral and scales cleanly from a 16-pixel favicon up to print.
